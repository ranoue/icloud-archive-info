# iCloud Photo Archive

A personal archive workflow operated by Reda. It uses rclone on a privately controlled Mac to upload original photo and video files to the owner's Google Drive and verify the uploaded copies. It is not offered to other users.

[Privacy policy](#privacy-policy) · [Terms of use](#terms-of-use)

## Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This workflow requests Google Drive permission to create, read, and manage files and folders created through this connection. It uses that access to store personal photo archives and verify their integrity.

Archive files are stored on the owner's Mac during processing and in the owner's Google Drive. Google authorization credentials are stored locally in the Mac's rclone configuration.

This public website contains information about the workflow. Photo archives and Google authorization credentials are not uploaded to this website or its GitHub repository.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. It is not intentionally shared with other people. Google processes data stored in Google Drive under its own terms and privacy policy.

The owner controls archive retention and deletion and can revoke the connection through their Google Account's third-party connections settings.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
